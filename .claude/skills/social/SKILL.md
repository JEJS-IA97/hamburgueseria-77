# Social Content

Use this workflow to plan organic social that builds audience, engagement, and business results. Stories and platform mechanics amplify a clear, authentic story; they do not replace one.

## Before planning

Check the Hamburguesería 77 project materials first: menu and promotions, business details, brand voice, current content, audience and available production time. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions. Use placeholders for unconfirmed prices, hours, promotion terms, delivery zones, availability, and customer claims. Do not invent them.

Clarify the primary objective (local awareness, inquiries/orders, community), target audience, voice, content capacity, and which past posts performed well. Ask only for information that is necessary to decide the work; otherwise mark it as an editable field.

## Content pillars

Use 3–5 pillars aligned to the local business and its real offer. Suitable starting pillars:

1. Food/product desire: real dishes, ingredients and preparation.
2. Behind the scenes: kitchen, service and people, with consent.
3. Local community: Labranza/Temuco, customer questions and interactions.
4. Practical ordering information: menu, location, delivery/pickup and how to order, using verified facts.
5. Promotions: only confirmed, with terms and dates visible.

Rotate pillars; avoid making every post a discount. Base future choices on the account's actual reach, shares, saves, profile visits, WhatsApp clicks, questions and attributed orders.

## Instagram and Facebook guidance

- Prefer native vertical video/Reels for discovery, carousels for a sequence or menu comparison, and Stories for timely updates and interaction with existing followers.
- Cadence is an experiment, not a universal rule. Begin with a sustainable schedule, then adjust to quality, capacity and account Insights. General reference: 1–2 feed posts and Stories daily; if that level is not feasible, choose a lower consistent cadence rather than low-quality volume.
- Use a strong visual, spoken and text hook in the opening moment of a Reel; show the real food/result early. Use subtitles, clear audio, and vertical 9:16 framing. No stock food imagery presented as the restaurant's own.
- For carousel, choose a narrative framework before drafting (e.g. product comparison or demo walkthrough). Slide 1 must work as a standalone hook; one idea per slide; one consistent template; final slide has one clear CTA. Use 4:5 (1080×1350) assets where appropriate and legible text.
- For Stories, use short sets with one purpose: show, interact, or help order. Use polls/questions only when genuine answers are useful. Spread frames into 2–3 batches instead of dumping a long sequence at once.
- Test time windows against Instagram's follower activity and actual post performance. Generic benchmarks are only a starting hypothesis; use the restaurant's local time zone and operating hours.
- Adapt copy for Facebook instead of mechanically copying. Use the local location context and a direct contact action. Reply to comments and messages when staff can handle resulting inquiries.
- Use a small number of relevant local/product hashtags; never let hashtags replace clear local keywords, location tags, or readable copy.

## Weekly calendar workflow

For each entry specify date/time and local timezone, pillar, format, goal, hook, visual/script, caption, CTA, required assets, and unverified fields. Schedule evergreen pieces; publish time-sensitive availability and promotion updates only after confirming they're true. Repurpose one filming session across Reels, Stories, and Facebook with platform-appropriate copy.

## Reel script template

- 0–2 sec: visual + spoken/text hook, with the finished dish or action visible.
- 2–5 sec: minimal context.
- 5–20 sec: preparation, ingredients or comparison, all based on real footage/facts.
- Final 3–5 sec: one CTA (e.g. message on WhatsApp), with confirmed contact/order info.

Keep each shot purposeful, use clean audio and subtitles, and avoid misleading editing or unverifiable claims. Respect image/customer consent.

## Measurement and iteration

Record at 24h, 48h, and 7d as relevant: reach/views, follower vs non-follower reach, watch/completion, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, contact-link clicks, DMs, inquiries and attributable orders. For Stories, compare first/last frame reach, replies, sticker/link interactions and completion. Likes alone are not a business outcome.

Weekly review: top and bottom pieces, their hook/pillar/format/time, useful actions, operating constraints and next test. Change one variable at a time when practical. Create drafts/calendar only; do not publish, message customers, or schedule posts on live accounts without explicit user authorization and a connected approved tool.
