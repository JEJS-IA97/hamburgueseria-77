# Marketing Plan

Use for a cross-channel, multi-period marketing plan, not for a single Instagram/Facebook content calendar. Structure recommendations around the customer journey: awareness/acquisition, first action, repeat behavior, referral, and revenue. Keep the plan customized to the real business, audience, budget, staff and operating capacity.

## Required grounding

Review project materials and any available channel, sales and customer data. Separate confirmed facts from estimates. Mark missing inputs explicitly; do not invent budget, conversion rates, sales, audience preferences, customer quotes or resource capacity. If a missing input materially changes the recommendation, ask one targeted question; otherwise use a clearly labeled assumption or editable field.

## Plan components

1. Executive summary with the few highest-priority bets.
2. Audience, offer and positioning from the available evidence.
3. Current state by channel: working, not working, unknown.
4. Prioritized acquisition, activation, retention, referral and revenue actions.
5. A sequenced near-term roadmap with responsible owner and effort.
6. Budget and capability limits; never assume ad spend is available.
7. Measurement: one north-star outcome, leading indicators and review cadence.
8. Open decisions and risks.

For each action name its mechanism, target audience, owner, effort, dependency, metric and stop/adjust criterion. Prefer a small number of executable experiments over an exhaustive tactic list. Brand/content support every stage rather than replacing a conversion path.

## Local restaurant adaptation

Prioritize practical local discovery and orders: accurate contact/location/hours, menu clarity, useful organic social, customer service, local search listings, reviews handled with permission, and trackable WhatsApp inquiries. Paid tactics require a budget and explicit approval. Confirm operational details before promising opening times, coverage, stock or offer terms.

## Scope boundary

The upstream source describes a comprehensive 12-month SaaS/fCMO engagement. Do not force its SaaS-specific AARRR sections, funding assumptions, long audit, or large idea bank onto a small local restaurant or a one-month social-calendar request. Use only the relevant planning principles.
