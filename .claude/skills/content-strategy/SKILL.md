# Content Strategy

Plan content that is useful, shareable, locally relevant and tied to a measurable business goal. Treat the calendar as a set of testable assets rather than a list of topics.

## Grounding and intake

Read the project's menu, promotion, brand and contact materials before ideation. Identify the target audience, goal, existing content, available formats, team capacity, questions customers ask, and any actual performance data. Label assumptions and use editable placeholders for missing information. Do not fabricate sales, bestsellers, customer feedback, hours, prices or promotion availability.

## Pillars and selection

Choose 3–5 durable content pillars that connect audience needs, real business strengths and production capacity. For a local restaurant, candidate pillars include product/food, preparation/people, local community, ordering information, and confirmed offers. Distribute content across them; don't turn every piece into a hard-sell.

Prioritize each idea by: customer relevance, fit with actual menu/brand, likely local discovery or sharing, and effort to produce. Prefer specific original footage, real menu facts, real questions and useful local details over generic trends. Searchable content answers a question; shareable content earns attention through a clear visual, story, useful comparison, or interaction.

## Distribution and repurposing

Create one strong source session and distribute it in adapted forms: a Reel for discovery, short Story clips/poll for community, a carousel when multiple steps/comparisons add value, and a Facebook version with a local CTA. Each adaptation must make sense on its own. Avoid posting identical repeated Story frames across channels.

## Calendar output

For each item include: date/time in the audience's timezone, pillar, audience need, format, hook, outline/script, required real assets, CTA, measurement signal and editable unknowns. Balance promotion with useful/relational content. Leave room for timely stock/availability updates, but verify before publication.

## Measurement

Track reach and non-follower discovery, retention/completion, shares/saves, profile visits, WhatsApp/link clicks, inquiries and attributable orders. Compare like with like (format, timing, topic) and use account analytics as the authority over generic benchmark studies. Run a small number of clearly described tests and revise the next calendar based on observed results.
